Privacy policy

This application is a private rclone client used only by its owner.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only on the owner's own computer, to sync and mount the owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
